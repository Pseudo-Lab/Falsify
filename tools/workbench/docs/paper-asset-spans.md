# Output asset spans

`resolveOutputAssetSpans({bytes, mupdf, flow, blockSpans})` returns `{spans, evidence}`. `bytes` are the compiled PDF, `mupdf` is the pinned MuPDF namespace, `flow` is the source-bound flow actually used to compile, and `blockSpans` are the compiler's start/end metadata. Do not supply source coordinates as output coordinates or invent flow IDs. A resolved entry uses the observed MuPDF image placement in display points, zero-based `pageIndex`, and both the render capture ID and its original asset IDs. The caller must bind the supplied flow and PDF to the same composition; the resolver cannot independently authenticate their provenance.

The marker nominates a page and the top/left and bottom of a block. Matching requires a single unrotated, opaque, in-page image at those anchors within 1.5 points, consistent source aspect ratio when available, no image overlap, no clipping of the observed image, no overlap between distinct source asset regions, and unique ownership. Rectangular clips that fully contain a placement are accepted; an image's intrinsic soft mask is recognized by MuPDF object identity and matching transform; other mask clips remain unavailable while active, and image masks are treated as overlapping paint when their bounds intersect the placement. Missing, duplicated, cross-page, stale or ambiguous nominations return no span. A shared capture's original IDs denote the whole capture, not individual panel geometry. Unsupported masks, clipping, vector artwork and multipanel placements may remain unavailable. The evidence records per-item outcome; it does not prove pixel identity, caption correctness, or visual quality.
